## What to watch each week (Insights)

- **Saves and shares:** the best signal of good content. Make more of whatever gets saved.
- **Link-in-bio clicks** and new emails on bloomko.eu: the real goal.
- **Watch time on reels:** if people drop off in the first 2 s, the hook is too slow.

After 4 weeks: take the reel with the most saves/shares and use it for the first paid promotion at launch (5–10 €/day).

✨ Meet your AI tutor

A tutor that knows exactly which words you know

Bloomko's AI tutor is built on Gemma, Google's open AI model, and runs entirely on your phone. It learns along with you — and never runs out of new things to practise.

  • Fresh sentences, every time — made only from words you've learned, focusing on the ones you get wrong
  • Smart grading — understands different but correct translations and explains what to fix
  • Memory hooks — a little story or picture to help every new word stick
  • Grows with you — sentences get harder as your level rises
  • Private and offline — no account, no cloud, your sentences never leave your phone

Translate into Chinese“I want to drink some tea.”
我要喝茶。
✓ Correct! Natural and clear. You could also say 我想喝点儿茶 — 想 sounds a bit softer than 要, and 点儿 means “a little”.
💡 Memory hook for 茶 (tea)A person 人 under a roof of grass 艹, resting among the trees 木 — with a cup of tea.

Learning that actually sticks

Most apps let you tap your way through a language. Bloomko makes you use it — with your hand, your voice and your memory.

✍️

Write from memory

A fresh page every time — no paper, no peeking at yesterday's attempts. Bloomko checks every stroke and its order, just like a patient teacher.

🧠

An AI tutor on your phone

Gemma, Google's open AI model, runs privately on your device: fresh sentences made from the words you know, translations graded with explanations, memory hooks for tricky words.

🎙️

Speak and be heard

Say words and whole sentences out loud and get instant feedback on your pronunciation — not just recognition, real speaking practice.

⏳

Reviews at the perfect moment

A modern memory model (FSRS) predicts when you're about to forget a word and brings it back just in time. Less repetition, more remembering.

🍃

Look-alike training

未 or 末? 己 or 已? Bloomko trains the characters that are easiest to mix up, side by side, until you can tell them apart at a glance.

🪴

A streak that grows

Your daily streak is a living plant. Skip a day and it gets thirsty — but a raindrop can save it. Gentle motivation, no guilt trips.

Your hand remembers what your eyes forget

Recognising a character is easy. Writing it from nothing is what makes it yours. Bloomko shows you the stroke order, lets you trace, and then asks you to write it from memory — and every mistake comes back until you get it right.

  • Animated stroke order for thousands of characters
  • Trace first, then write it all by yourself
  • Wrong answers repeat until they're right

A garden instead of a scoreboard

Bloomko is designed to feel calm. No flashing confetti, no pushy notifications — just a forest-green space where your knowledge grows leaf by leaf.

  • A daily plan that tells you exactly what's left — and how long it takes
  • Raindrops that protect your streak on busy days
  • A friendly leaf-shaped reminder, only on days you haven't practised

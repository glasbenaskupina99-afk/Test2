✨ Meet your AI tutor

Talk, ask, practise — with a tutor in your pocket

Bloomko's tutor is built on Gemma, Google's open AI model, and runs entirely on your phone — even in airplane mode. It only uses the words you've already learned, so every conversation is just the right level.

  • Role-play chats — order at a café, ask the way, meet someone new. Answer by voice or typing; gentle corrections when something's off
  • It listens to you — pronunciation and tone feedback, with your voice's pitch drawn next to the real tone
  • Fresh sentences and stories — made only from words you know, focusing on the ones you get wrong
  • Memory hooks — a little picture or story for every new word
  • Private — your conversations never leave your phone
